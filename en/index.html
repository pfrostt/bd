How it works

  1. Fill in the form. A few lines are enough.
  2. I’ll get back to you within a day.
  3. We’ll have a chat. You can tell me what you have in mind.
  4. We’ll arrange the shoot for a time and place that suits you.
  5. The photos are yours. Nothing is published without your consent.

Do I need any experience?

No. Most people have never done anything like this before. I’ll guide you through the entire shoot.

Who will see the photos?

Just you and me. You decide if anyone else ever gets to see them.

What should I wear?

Whatever makes you feel beautiful and comfortable. We’ll talk about it during our chat.

Where does the shoot take place?

Wherever suits you, in Malmö, Lund, or elsewhere in Skåne.

How much does a boudoir photoshoot cost?

The price depends on the setup. Get in touch and I’ll tell you more about the options.

You’ll receive your finished photos within a day.
